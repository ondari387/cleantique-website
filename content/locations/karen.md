## Bigger compounds, different pest pressure

Karen's plots tend to be larger and greener than most of Nairobi, often backing onto forest or open land. That means more potential entry points and breeding ground for mosquitoes, rodents and other pests than a typical in-town apartment or house.

## Pest control for compounds

We inspect the full compound, not just the main house, and treat according to what we find - cockroaches and rodents indoors, mosquitoes in the garden - with the product and re-entry time given to you in writing beforehand. See [pest control & fumigation](/services/pest-control/), [rodent control](/services/pest-control/rodent-control/) and [mosquito control](/services/pest-control/mosquito-control/).

## Cleaning for larger properties

Deep cleaning a Karen compound often includes staff quarters, outbuildings and larger living areas than a standard house. Our [cleaning](/services/cleaning/) service is scoped on-site so the quote reflects the actual property, not a flat per-room rate.

## Moving from or into Karen

Larger households and longer driveways change how a move is planned - access for the truck, timing, and the volume of goods. We survey before quoting; see [home moving](/services/moving-relocation/home-moving/).
