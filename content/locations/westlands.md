Westlands mixes high-rise offices, retail centres and dense apartment living, often in the same block. That changes what a cleaning or pest control job actually involves: an office needs cleaning scheduled around business hours without disrupting staff, while an apartment block needs pest control that accounts for shared walls and corridors, not just one unit.
