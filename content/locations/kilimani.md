## Pest control in apartment blocks

Bed bugs and cockroaches travel between units through shared walls, risers and corridors, which makes multi-unit buildings in Kilimani a different problem from a standalone house. We inspect before we treat, explain the product and re-entry time in writing, and include a follow-up visit.
