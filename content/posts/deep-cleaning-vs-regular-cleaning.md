When people book a cleaning service, the most common source of disappointment is booking the wrong type of clean. A regular clean on a home that has not had proper attention for months will improve it, but it will not reach the grease on the cabinet fronts or the limescale on the taps. A deep clean every week, on the other hand, is more than most homes need.

This guide explains the difference, room by room, and helps you decide which your home needs.

## The short version

- **A regular clean maintains** a home that is already in good condition. It focuses on the surfaces and rooms that get dirty every week.
- **A deep clean resets** a home. It reaches built-up dirt, grease and limescale in places that regular cleaning skips.

Most homes benefit from a deep clean first, then a regular schedule to keep that standard.

## Room by room

| Area | Regular clean | Deep clean |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen | Wipe worktops, hob and sink; clean appliance exteriors; mop floor; empty bins | Everything in a regular clean, plus degreasing cabinet fronts and splashbacks, cleaning behind and under appliances, extractor hood, and optionally inside the oven, fridge and cupboards |
| Bathrooms | Clean toilet, basin, shower and bath; wipe mirrors; mop floor | Everything in a regular clean, plus limescale removal from taps, showerheads and glass, scrubbing tile grout, cleaning extractor fans |
| Bedrooms | Dust surfaces; vacuum or mop floors; make beds on request | Dust tops of wardrobes, skirting, door frames and light fittings; clean under beds and furniture; wipe switches and handles |
| Living areas | Dust surfaces; vacuum and mop; tidy cushions | Clean under and behind furniture, skirting, light fittings, window sills and frames; optionally upholstery |
| Throughout | Floors and surfaces | Interior windows, door frames, light switches, cobwebs from ceiling corners, high-level dusting |

## Signs your home needs a deep clean

- It has been more than a few months since a thorough clean.
- Grease has built up on kitchen cabinets, tiles or the extractor hood.
- Taps, showerheads and glass have white limescale deposits.
- Dust is visible on skirting, door frames, tops of wardrobes and light fittings.
- There are dark lines in tile grout.
- You are moving in or out, preparing for guests or an event, or starting a regular cleaning schedule.
- Someone in the home has been ill.
- There has been building or painting work (although heavy construction dust needs a [post-construction clean](/services/cleaning/post-construction-cleaning/)).

## Signs a regular clean is enough

- The home has had a deep clean recently.
- Kitchens and bathrooms are cleaned weekly.
- You mainly want help with weekly upkeep so that you have more free time.

## How often to book each

| Household | Regular cleaning | Deep cleaning |
|---|---|---|
| Busy family with children or pets | Weekly | Every three to four months |
| Couple or single person in a flat | Fortnightly | Twice a year |
| Home with a full-time house help | Not usually needed | Two to four times a year, focusing on areas that daily cleaning misses |
| Short-term rental | After every stay (turnover clean) | Every few months, or between long bookings |

These are starting points. Dust from nearby roads and construction, the rainy season and cooking habits all affect how quickly a home gets dirty.

## Why Nairobi homes get dirty in particular ways

- **Dust:** in the dry season especially, dust from roads and building sites collects on window sills, tracks and surfaces.
- **Mud:** during the rains, floors and entrances need more attention.
- **Hard water:** limescale builds up on taps, showerheads and kettles in many areas.
- **Cooking:** frying and deep frying leave grease on cabinets, walls and extractor hoods.

A deep clean every few months deals with this build-up so that regular cleaning stays quick and effective.

## How long each takes

- A regular clean of a two-bedroom flat in good condition might take two to three hours with one or two people.
- A deep clean of the same flat often takes three to six hours with a small team, depending on condition and extras.

## The best-value approach

1. **Start with a deep clean** to bring the home up to standard.
2. **Move to a regular schedule** to maintain it.
3. **Add a deep clean every few months**, timed for the dusty dry season or before the holidays.
4. **Use extras selectively**, such as the oven or fridge, when they need it rather than every time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a spring clean the same as a deep clean?

Broadly, yes. A spring clean is a seasonal deep clean, often combined with decluttering.

### Does a deep clean include carpets and sofas?

Not usually as standard. Upholstery, carpet and mattress cleaning are specialist tasks that can be added.

### Will a deep clean remove mould?

Light surface mould on tiles and grout can be cleaned. Recurrent or extensive mould usually has an underlying damp problem that needs fixing, and may need a specialist.

### Can I book a deep clean while I am at home?

Yes. Many clients stay home, though it is easier for the team if you can keep out of the rooms they are working in.
