If you have asked for transport quotes in Kenya, you have probably noticed that prices for what looks like the same job can vary widely. That is because the price of transporting goods depends on much more than the distance between two points.

This guide explains what drives the cost of goods transport, so you can understand quotes, compare them fairly and find ways to reduce what you pay. We have not listed rates, because they change with fuel prices, demand and route conditions, and a number without context can mislead.

## The main cost factors

### 1. Distance and route

Longer routes use more fuel and driver time. But route matters as much as distance:

- **Traffic.** A short trip across Nairobi at peak hours can take longer than a longer trip on an open road.
- **Road conditions.** Rough roads slow vehicles and increase wear.
- **Tolls and charges** on some routes and in some areas.

### 2. Weight and volume

These decide the size of vehicle needed. Bulky but light goods, such as furniture or packaging, can fill a vehicle before they reach its weight limit. Dense goods, such as cement or tiles, may reach the weight limit while the vehicle still looks half empty. A carrier prices for whichever limit your load reaches first.

### 3. Type of vehicle

From a small pickup to a large truck, the vehicle affects the price. Specialist vehicles, such as those with tail lifts, cranes or refrigeration, cost more and are not always available.

### 4. Loading and offloading

| Situation | Effect on cost |
|---|---|
| Loading bay and forklift at both ends | Quickest and simplest |
| Manual loading of cartons | More people and time |
| Heavy or awkward items | Extra people or equipment |
| Stairs or long carries at either end | Significant extra time |
| Waiting for goods to be ready | Waiting time may be charged |

### 5. Number of drops

A single collection and delivery is simplest. Multi-drop routes, such as stock to several shops, take longer because of stops, offloading and paperwork at each.

### 6. Timing and urgency

Same-day and urgent jobs, early morning or late-night deliveries, weekend work and month-end peaks can all affect the price. Flexible timing usually costs less.

### 7. Return trips

If the vehicle returns empty from a long trip, the cost of that return journey is usually reflected in the price. If a return load is available, the cost can be shared.

### 8. Frequency

Regular, scheduled runs allow a carrier to plan vehicles and drivers efficiently, which can make each run better value than ad hoc bookings.

### 9. Goods requiring special handling

Fragile, high-value or temperature-sensitive goods need more care, specialised equipment or specialist carriers, and are priced accordingly.

## How a transport quote is built

A carrier needs to know:

1. **What the goods are** and how they are packed.
2. **Weight and volume**, or dimensions and number of pieces.
3. **Collection and delivery addresses**, and access at each.
4. **Collection date and delivery deadline.**
5. **Loading and offloading** arrangements.
6. **Number of drops.**
7. **Any special handling.**

The more accurately you describe the job, the more accurate the quote, and the less chance of surprises on the day.

## Ways to reduce transport costs

- **Consolidate loads.** One larger delivery usually costs less than several small ones.
- **Pack efficiently.** Palletise and stack goods to make best use of space.
- **Be ready on time.** Having goods packed and staff available avoids waiting time.
- **Be flexible on timing.** Avoid peak periods where you can.
- **Plan routes.** Group deliveries in the same area.
- **Set up a regular schedule** if you transport goods often.
- **Give accurate details** so the right vehicle is sent first time.

## Hidden costs to ask about

- Waiting time beyond an agreed period.
- Loading and offloading charges.
- Charges for stairs or long carries.
- Fuel surcharges.
- Extra drops added on the day.
- Return trips for undeliverable goods.

Ask what is included and what is extra before you book.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is transport priced per kilometre?

Some carriers use per-kilometre rates, others price per trip or per load. In all cases, weight, volume, access and timing affect the final price.

### Why is a short trip in Nairobi sometimes expensive?

Traffic, waiting time, loading and offloading can take longer than the drive itself. A short distance does not always mean a short job.

### How can I get an accurate quote quickly?

Send a description of the goods, approximate weight or number of pieces, photos, both addresses, access details and your timing.

### Do I need insurance?

For valuable goods, check what cover the carrier provides and consider your own goods-in-transit insurance.
