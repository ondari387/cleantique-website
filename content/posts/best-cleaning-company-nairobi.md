## There is no single "best" cleaning company

A one-off deep clean for a two-bedroom flat, a scheduled office contract, and a post-construction handover clean are three different jobs with different equipment, timing and risk. A company that excels at one is not automatically the right choice for another. This guide compares Nairobi's highest-rated cleaning companies by what they are actually known for.
