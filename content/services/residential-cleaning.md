## Regular cleaning, deep cleaning or a move-in or move-out clean?

**A deep clean is the reset.** It reaches the built-up dirt that everyday cleaning misses: grease on cabinet fronts and splashbacks, limescale on taps and showers, dust on skirting, door frames and light fittings, and dirt under and behind furniture. Book it when a home has not had proper attention for a while, before a special occasion, or before you start a regular schedule.

**A regular clean keeps that standard.** Kitchens, bathrooms, floors, surfaces and bins, weekly or fortnightly. It is much quicker because the built-up work has already been done.

**A move-in or move-out clean** is a deep clean of an empty property. Empty is important: we can reach every surface, inside every cupboard and behind every appliance, and the job takes less time than a deep clean of a furnished home. A move-out clean is aimed squarely at the landlord's inspection; a move-in clean means you unpack into a genuinely clean home.

**Short-term rental turnovers** sit somewhere in between. They need to be fast, consistent and photograph-ready, with bed making and restocking if you want it.

## Carpet, upholstery and window cleaning

These are genuine specialities within our residential cleaning service, not a vague add-on. Sofas, carpets and window glass carry grime that ordinary dusting and vacuuming do not remove, and Nairobi's dust and road grime make the difference obvious within weeks.

- **Carpet cleaning.** Machine extraction lifts ground-in dirt, stains and dust that regular vacuuming leaves behind, and carpets dry faster than most people expect.
- **Upholstery cleaning.** Sofas, armchairs and dining chairs are steam cleaned to lift dust, body oils and stains from the fabric, without soaking the foam underneath.
- **Window cleaning.** Interior glass and sills are part of every deep clean. Exterior windows, including upper floors, can be added where access allows.

Any of these can be booked as a standalone visit or added to a deep clean. Tell us which rooms and pieces need attention when you book.

## What is standard and what needs extra arrangements

Standard cleaning covers surfaces we can safely reach from the floor or a small step ladder. These need separate arrangements:

- **Heavy furniture.** We clean under and behind furniture that can be moved safely. Wardrobes and heavy units stay in place unless agreed.
- **High-level and exterior work.** External windows above ground floor and high ceilings need extra equipment.
- **Specialist surfaces.** Natural stone, unsealed wood and antique furniture may need specific products.
- **Laundry, ironing and dishes.** Available by arrangement, not part of a standard clean.
- **Heavy soiling.** Extreme grease, mould or waste build-up is assessed and quoted separately.

## How residential cleaning works

1. **Describe the home.** Number of bedrooms and bathrooms, current condition, which service you need, and any extras. Photos help.
2. **Agree the checklist.** You receive the list of what is included and what has been added.
3. **Prepare.** Clear clutter and put valuables away. We send a short note before the first visit.
4. **We clean.** The team works through the list, room by room, top to bottom.
5. **We check.** The work is reviewed against the list before the team leaves. If something in scope was missed, we come back and fix it at no extra cost.

## Preparing your home for a clean

- Pick up clothes, toys and papers from floors and surfaces.
- Empty the sink of dishes unless washing up has been added.
- Put away jewellery, cash, documents and fragile ornaments.
- Tell us about delicate surfaces, pets, and rooms we should not enter.
- Confirm water, electricity and parking or gate access.

## Equipment and supplies

The team brings vacuum cleaners, mops, microfibre cloths, scrubbing tools, ladders and a range of cleaning products suited to kitchens, bathrooms and general surfaces. If you prefer specific products, for allergies, natural stone or simply preference, leave them out and we will use yours.

## Areas that need extra attention

- **Kitchens:** grease on cabinet fronts, extractor hoods and splashbacks builds up quickly with Kenyan cooking.
- **Bathrooms:** hard water in parts of Nairobi leaves limescale on taps, showerheads and glass.
- **Window sills and tracks:** dust from roads and construction gathers here, especially in the dry season.
- **Skirting and door frames:** rarely cleaned but very visible once they are.

## Safety and care

We test products on a small area before using them on delicate surfaces, never mix chemicals, and ventilate as we work. If you have children or pets, tell us and we will keep products out of reach and use milder options where possible.

## Common mistakes

- Booking a regular clean for a home that needs a deep clean first, then being disappointed with the result.
- Assuming extras such as the oven are included without asking.
- Booking a move-out clean while furniture is still in place.
- Not mentioning a marble or granite surface until after it has been cleaned.
