## 8.3 New blog posts recommended

These capture strong informational-intent clusters from your list that don't fit any existing post, each linking back to the relevant service page per the internal-linking plan in §2.6:

1. **"Does fumigation kill cockroaches, rats, ants, fleas and bed bugs?"** — one consolidated, honest post covering the "does fumigation kill X" cluster (roaches, rats, mice, ants, fleas, bed bugs). Also answers "does fumigation kill snakes" honestly: no — snakes aren't a chemical-fumigation target, and saying so plainly is a small trust signal, not a weakness.
2. **"What to do after fumigation, and when it's safe to come home"** — covers "after fumigation what to do" / "after fumigation returning home", a real pre-booking anxiety point for residential customers.
3. **"Do you need a fumigation certificate in Kenya?"** — covers "fumigation certificate" / "fumigation certificate kenya". Strong B2B trust angle: landlords, lease agreements, hospitality and export buyers often require one. Link from the pest-control hub.
4. **"End of lease fumigation: why some Nairobi landlords require it when you move out"** — covers "end of lease fumigation" / "end of lease pet fumigation". This is a genuine differentiation opportunity: it's the natural bridge between your moving and pest-control divisions, ties directly into the existing `move-in-move-out-cleaning` post, and is exactly the kind of cross-service content the strategy doc already identifies as your real edge (§2.2, takeaway 3).
5. **"Nuvan and other DIY fumigation foggers: what they can and can't do"** — covers "nuvan profi fumigation fogger" / "nuvan fumigation fogger". An honest DIY-vs-professional comparison. This captures people already trying to solve the problem themselves and converts the ones for whom a DIY fogger isn't enough (the common case with established infestations).

A sixth option — "is fumigation the same as fogging?" — is better handled as a short FAQ entry directly on `/services/pest-control/mosquito-control/` rather than a separate post; the term doesn't have enough depth to justify its own page and it belongs right where the buyer is already reading about fogging.

## 8.5 The one thing to actively avoid

Several keywords in your list — "best fumigation company in Kenya," "best fumigation company in Nairobi," "best movers near me," "best cleaning company" — are tempting to chase by just writing "best fumigation company" into headings repeatedly. **Don't.** That's the literal weakness the competitor audit in §2.2 already identified in bestcareservices.co.ke ("unsupported superlatives") and the brand's own design system explicitly forbids self-declared superlatives and unstated claims.

You rank for "best X" queries the honest way: by being the page that most completely and clearly answers the buyer's actual questions (what's included, is it safe, what affects the price, how it works), with genuine cross-service depth nobody else in the Nairobi market has combined well. That's already the stated strategy in §2.2 — this keyword list doesn't change that, it just adds more specific long-tail terms underneath it.
