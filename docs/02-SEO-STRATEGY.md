## 2.1 What we could and could not research

- **Done:** live web searches (October 2026) for the priority terms; review of the pages that came up for them; reading of the existing Cleantique pages; public-health and regulatory sources for technical claims.
- **Not available:** keyword-volume, difficulty and ranking tools (Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush), Google Search Console and GA4 data for the current site. **No search volumes, difficulty scores or ranking positions are quoted in this document.** Once Search Console is connected, use its Queries report to validate and reprioritise the map below.

## 2.2 Competitive landscape (observed)

Searches for "fumigation services Nairobi", "bed bug fumigation Nairobi cost", "cleaning companies Nairobi", "house movers Nairobi", "post construction cleaning Nairobi", "cockroach control Nairobi", "office relocation Nairobi" and "mosquito control Nairobi" returned results dominated by two domains:

| Site | What they do well | Weaknesses observed |
|---|---|---|
| achisystems.co.ke | Very broad coverage: directory pages, "X providers in Nairobi" lists, location × service pages | Thin, templated pages; repeated phrasing; directory format rather than first-hand service detail |
| bestcareservices.co.ke | Many service and location pages; KES price tables on some pages; clear phone CTAs | Repetitive keyword use ("Nairobi and Kenya"), unsupported superlatives, broad unsourced price ranges, placeholder links, inconsistent address |
| commercialpropertykenya.com (guide) | Comparison format, buyer checklist and red flags; price ranges by home size | Unsourced price figures and ratings, no ranking criteria, no disclosure |
| Transport searches (fuzu.com, goodfirms.co, directories) | Company listings | Few genuine service pages from operators; low competition for helpful local content |

**Takeaways**

1. Rankings in this niche are currently won by volume of pages rather than depth. That leaves room for a site with fewer, genuinely useful pages that answer the buyer's real questions (preparation, what is included, what affects price, realistic expectations).
2. Competitors publish price tables with wide, unsourced ranges. We deliberately do not invent prices; instead each pricing article explains the factors and how a fair quote is built, which is more defensible and still matches "cost" intent. If the owner later wants to publish genuine starting prices, add them to the pricing articles and service pages.
3. Nobody combines all four services well. Cross-service combinations (move + clean + fumigate) are a genuine differentiator and are reflected in the homepage, `/services/` and internal links.
4. Logistics search results show few helpful operator pages, so the transport guides have a realistic chance of ranking for informational terms.

## 2.6 Internal linking plan

- **Header mega menu** links every division and child page from every page.
- **Footer** links all service pages, company pages and legal pages.
- **Parent → child:** each division page shows cards for its child services.
- **Child → parent:** breadcrumbs plus a division label in the hero.
- **Service → related services:** 3 related services per page (cross-division where useful, e.g. bed bugs → residential cleaning, home moving).
- **Service → articles:** 3 related articles per service page.
- **Article → service:** every article links in its body to the relevant service page(s), has a sidebar service card and a "Related services" section, and ends with a service-specific quote link (`/request-quote/?service=…&type=…`) that preselects the form.
- **Article → article:** 3 related articles per post plus in-body links.
- **Pricing articles** are linked from each service page's "What affects the price" section.
- **Anchor text** is descriptive ("bed bug control", "house moving checklist"), never "click here".

## 2.7 Location page strategy

The prompt lists 16 candidate areas. We have **not** created location pages, because:

1. Cleantique's service coverage for each area has not been confirmed in detail (response times, frequency of work there, local examples).
2. Without genuine local information, 16 areas × 13 services would produce near-identical doorway pages, which Google's spam policies specifically target, and which is exactly the weakness of the competing directory sites.

**Recommended approach (phase 2):**

- Create a location page only where Cleantique can provide at least: real jobs completed there, typical property types and access issues (e.g. Kilimani high-rise lifts, Karen large compounds and mosquitoes, Syokimau/Kitengela new builds), local photos, and a customer review from that area.
- Start with the 3–4 areas with the most real jobs (from the enquiries inbox and Search Console impressions).
- One page per area covering all services (`/areas/kilimani/`), not one page per service per area.
- Each area page must have unique content of genuine value; if it would be mostly the same as another area page, do not publish it.

Until then, each service page has an "Areas we serve" section and the LocalBusiness schema lists the service areas.

## 2.8 Content quality principles applied

- No invented prices, statistics, certifications, awards, employee numbers, years in business or ratings.
- Technical pest claims follow public guidance (US EPA bed bug preparation guidance; Kenya PCPB for product regulation; Kenyan reporting on *Aedes* mosquitoes and arboviruses).
- Limitations are stated plainly: fogging is short-term; bed bug eggs survive first treatments; deposits depend on more than cleaning; general goods only.
- No hazardous application instructions (e.g. rodent bait placement) are published.
- FAQs are written to help, with FAQPage markup present, but no rich result is promised.
